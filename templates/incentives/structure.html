How incentive is earned

Edit rules & slabs

Every approved sale earns points, and a point is a rupee. Two things can pay: a base rate on the sale itself, and a ladder driven by how much the seller has already done in the period. The calculator at the bottom runs the same code that pays a real sale.

Rules that apply to everything

  • Only approved sales count toward a ladder. A rejected sale earns nothing and is left out of everyone's running total.
  • Changing a rule does not rewrite sales already saved — it applies to sales entered from then on.
  • A running total resets when its period does: on the 1st for monthly ladders, on 1 April for financial-year ladders.
  • An active campaign on a product replaces its regular incentive for the campaign's dates.
