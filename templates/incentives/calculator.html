A 2 or 3 year policy — the client pays for every year up front, but you are not paid for all of it at once. The premium is split by the number of years, and you earn one year at a time:
  • Year 1 lands with the sale.
  • Year 2 lands on the policy's anniversary next year, on that date.
  • Year 3 lands on the anniversary the year after.

There is nothing to collect and nothing to enter — the money is already paid, so the policy renews itself and the points simply arrive. Each year is priced by the step your month has reached when it lands, so a good month lifts it too. Anything owed to you shows in Points already coming to you above.

Worth knowing

  • Points are counted only once a sale is approved. A rejected sale earns nothing.
  • Your steps reset with their period — monthly ones on the 1st, the yearly one on 1 April.
  • A sale keeps the points it was given. If a rate changes later, past sales are not rewritten.
  • During a campaign, that product's campaign terms replace the ones here for the campaign dates.
